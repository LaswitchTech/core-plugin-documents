The Consultant will receive a fee equivalent to {{rate}}% of the amounts successfully recovered. Fees are payable to the Consultant upon receipt by the Client of credits or refunds issued by governing authorities and other 3rd-parties. If no recovery is made, no fees or expenses will be charged.

3. Amendments and Additional Services

The parties agree to consult with each other immediately prior to the commencement of the review process to determine in writing any work already underway that is to be specifically excluded from this Agreement. Additionally, any potential services beyond the scope of this Agreement, such as appeals before the Canadian International Trade Tribunal (C.I.T.T.) or other specialized services, will require a separate agreement clearly defining such services and applicable rates.

4. Confidentiality Commitment

Confidentiality obligations shall be governed by any existing Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) executed between the parties. If no separate NDA exists, the Consultant undertakes to keep all information confidential.

 

5. Duration

This Agreement remains in effect for both parties until all recovery actions related to duties paid on declarations made prior to any final decision date on such declarations covered under this Agreement have been fully processed by federal authorities, including the expiry of any applicable legislative period for appeals or reconsiderations.
